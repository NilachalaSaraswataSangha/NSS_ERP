# NSS ERP Mahila Sangha Module Overview

Version: 1.0
Status: DRAFT

---

# 1. Purpose

The Mahila Sangha Module manages participation, organizational association, activities, branch structure, central supervision, governance representation, and reporting of Mahila Sangha within NSS.

Mahila Sangha is an official NSS institution.

---

# 2. Constitutional Position

Mahila Sangha is an official organizational institution of NSS.
Formation approved and encouraged by Swami Nigamananda Paramahansa Dev.
First Mahila Sangha in Odisha recorded as having started in 1948.

---

# 3. Membership Principle

Mahila Sangha does not create a separate NSS Membership system.
A woman participating in Mahila Sangha remains an NSS Member under common framework.
Membership Types remain: PROBATIONARY, REGULAR, ASSOCIATE.
Identity remains: Sangha Sevi ID.
No separate Mahila Member ID.

---

# 4. Organizational Structure

Kendra Mahila Sangha -> Mahila Parichalana Mandali -> Mahila Sakha Sanghas.
Mahila Parichalana Mandali is the central supervisory body.

---

# 5. Mahila Leadership (Project-Frozen)

9 members. Positions: President, Vice President, Secretary, Assistant Secretary, Treasurer, Members.
Note: Project-frozen rule, not confirmed as Bye-Law constitutional fact.

---

# 6. Governance Reuse

Uses Unified Body Governance Model (body_master, body_member_assignment, position_master).
Body type: MAHILA_PARICHALANA_MANDALI.
No separate governance tables for Mahila.

---

# 7. Mahila Activities

May include: Mahila Puja, Training, Service, Educational, Cultural, Spiritual, Organizational activities.
Mahila Puja is a separate operational domain from Mahila Sangha organizational structure.

---

# 8. Items Requiring Authoritative Confirmation

Must not be presented as constitutional fact unless confirmed:
- Exact tenure of Mahila Parichalana Mandali
- Exact election/selection mechanism
- Exact central body composition
- Whether separate Mahila General Body exists
- Mahila-specific constitutional powers differing from common NSS framework

---

# 9. Frozen Decisions

- Mahila Sangha is an official NSS institution
- Uses common NSS Membership framework
- No separate Mahila Membership category
- No separate Mahila renewal process
- Sangha Sevi ID remains Membership identity
- Mahila Parichalana Mandali is central supervisory body
- Mahila Sakha Sanghas exist
- Unified Body Governance Model reused
- History preserved, never deleted
