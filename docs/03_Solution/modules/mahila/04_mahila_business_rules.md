Status: DRAFT

# 1. Institutional Rules

## MAH-001 — Official NSS Institution
Mahila Sangha is an official NSS organizational institution.

## MAH-002 — Central Supervisory Body
Mahila Parichalana Mandali is the central supervisory body.

## MAH-003 — Branch Structure
Supports branch-level Mahila Sanghas associated with Sakhas.

# 2. Membership Rules

## MAH-005 — Common Membership Framework
Uses common NSS Membership framework.

## MAH-006 — No Separate Membership Type
No MAHILA_MEMBER type. Types remain: PROBATIONARY, REGULAR, ASSOCIATE.

## MAH-007 — Sangha Sevi Identity
Sangha Sevi ID is the Membership identity.

## MAH-008 — No Separate Renewal
No separate Mahila renewal process.

## MAH-009 — Common Transfer
Common NSS transfer process applies.
