# NSS ERP Kumari Sangha Business Rules

Document ID: SOL-KUM-004
Status: DRAFT

---

# 1. Identity Rules

## KUM-001 — Kumari Is Not NSS Membership
Kumari participation shall not be treated as NSS Membership.

## KUM-002 — Kumari ID
Every Kumari participant shall receive a Kumari ID. Example: KM000123

## KUM-003 — Kumari ID Permanence
Unique, Permanent, Never Reused, Preserved after exit.

## KUM-004 — Sangha Sevi ID Separation
A Kumari participant shall not automatically receive a Sangha Sevi ID.

---

# 2. Person Rules

## KUM-005 — Person Foundation
Every Kumari participant shall be represented by a Person record.

## KUM-006 — Non-Member Person
A Kumari participant may exist without Sangha Sevi ID, Membership, Parichaya Patra, Anumati Patra.

---

# 3. Family Rules

## KUM-007 — Family Integration
Where applicable, a Kumari participant shall be associated with the relevant Family.

## KUM-008 — Family Relationship Ownership
Family relationships remain owned by the Family Module.

---

# 4. Sakha Rules

## KUM-009 — Sakha Association
A Kumari participant shall be associated with the relevant Sakha.

## KUM-010 — Sakha Visibility
Authorized Sakha users may view participants belonging to their Sakha.

## KUM-011 — Kendra Visibility
Authorized Kendra users may view Kumari participants across Sakhas.

---

# 5. Participation Rules

## KUM-012 — Continuous Program
Kumari Sangha is a continuing developmental institution/program.

## KUM-013 — Activity History
Kumari activities shall be historically recorded.

## KUM-014 — Training History
Training history shall be preserved.

---

# 6. Activity Rules

## KUM-015 — Activity Participation
Participation in Kumari activities shall be recorded.

## KUM-016 — Dina-Lipi
Dina-Lipi participation/tracking may be maintained.

## KUM-017 — Niyam Panchak
Niyam Panchak participation/training may be maintained.

## KUM-018 — Dasa Sheela
Dasa Sheela participation/training may be maintained.

---

# 7. Marriage Rules

## KUM-019 — Active Kumari Eligibility
Only unmarried girls may remain active participants.

## KUM-020 — Marriage Exit
After marriage, status becomes MARRIED_OUT.

## KUM-021 — Marriage History
Marriage-related exit shall not delete the Kumari record.

---

# 8. Other Exit Rules

## KUM-022 — Withdrawal
WITHDRAWN status for voluntary exit.

## KUM-023 — Deceased
DECEASED status where applicable.

## KUM-024 — Membership Transition Status
BECAME_NSS_MEMBER when participant becomes an NSS Member.

---

# 9. NSS Membership Transition

## KUM-025 — Application
A Kumari participant may apply for NSS Membership.

## KUM-026 — No Automatic Membership
Kumari participation shall never automatically create NSS Membership.

## KUM-027 — Membership Assessment
Membership authorities may consider Kumari history during assessment.

## KUM-028 — Regular Membership Consideration
Long-term active participant may be considered for direct Regular Membership.

## KUM-029 — Probationary Membership Consideration
May be considered for Probationary Membership where assessment requires it.

## KUM-030 — Assessment Inputs
Years of participation, Dina-Lipi, Niyam Panchak, Dasa Sheela, Training, Conduct, Discipline, Seva, Leadership recommendation. These do not themselves grant Membership.

---

# 10. Transition History

## KUM-031 — Transition Record
Every approved transition shall be historically recorded.

## KUM-032 — Identity Link
Transition preserves Kumari ID + Sangha Sevi ID.

## KUM-033 — No Identity Replacement
Sangha Sevi ID shall not replace the historical Kumari ID.

---

# 11. Audit and Deletion

## KUM-036 — Auditability
Kumari actions shall preserve applicable audit information.

## KUM-038 — Physical Deletion Prohibited
Kumari participation history shall not be physically deleted.

## KUM-039 — Status-Based Lifecycle
Exit represented through status and reason, not physical deletion.

---

# 12. Frozen Principles

- Kumari Participation is not NSS Membership
- Every Kumari Participant Has Kumari ID
- Kumari ID is not Sangha Sevi ID
- Sangha Sevi ID Generated Only After Membership Approval
- Kumari History Is Preserved
- Marriage Is Explicit Exit Condition
- Long-Term Participants May Be Considered for Membership
- Family Integration Uses the Family Module
- Physical Deletion Is Prohibited
