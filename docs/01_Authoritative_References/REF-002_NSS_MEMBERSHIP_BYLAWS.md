# REF-002-002 — Probationary Members

## Subject

Probationary Members

---

### Source Reference

REF-SRC-002

---

### Constitutional Provision

#### Clause 1

A person seeking enrolment as a Probationary Member shall possess good moral character and shall have accepted Shri Shri Thakur as the sole and supreme Guru and Ista.

#### Clause 2

The applicant shall observe the Niyam Panchak in the manner prescribed by the Governing Body.

#### Clause 3

The applicant shall abide by the rules and regulations contained in the Sangha Neeti promulgated by the Kendra Sangha from time to time.

#### Clause 4

The applicant shall resolve to dedicate himself or herself as a Sevak of Shri Shri Thakur, His devotees, and the Sangha.

#### Clause 5

The applicant shall be leading a household life with the objective of developing it into an Adarsha Grihastha Jeevan as enunciated by Shri Shri Thakur.

#### Clause 6

Upon enrolment as a Probationary Member and completion of the prescribed ceremonies, the member shall be issued an Anumati Patra signed and issued by the Kendra Sangha.

#### Clause 7

A Probationary Member shall conduct himself or herself in accordance with the directions contained in the Anumati Patra, recognize the sanctity of Saraswata Matha and Guru Dhama, and maintain allegiance to Nilachala Kutir, Smruti Mandir at Puri, and Sikshya Kendra at Biratung.

#### Clause 8

A Probationary Member is entitled to vote in matters relating to the concerned Sakha Sangha and in the General Body meeting of the Kendra Sangha but is ordinarily not eligible to hold the office of President or Secretary of a Sakha Sangha.

#### Clause 9

Under special circumstances, a Probationary Member may function as President or Secretary of a Sakha Sangha with the approval of the Parichalak. Such approval shall not constitute a precedent.

# REF-002-003 — Regular Members

## Subject

Regular Members

---

### Source Reference

REF-SRC-002

---

### Constitutional Provision

#### Clause 1

To become a Regular Member, a person shall have continued as a Probationary Member of a Sakha Sangha, including a Mahila Sangha, while holding a valid Anumati Patra for at least one year.

#### Clause 2

On the recommendation of the Sakha Sangha, the member shall have undergone training under the guidance of the Kendra Sangha for at least one year.

#### Clause 3

After successful completion of the prescribed training, the member may be enrolled as a Regular Member of the Kendra Sangha and shall be issued a Parichaya Patra by the Kendra Sangha.

#### Clause 4

A Regular Member shall actively participate in all activities undertaken by the Kendra Sangha in addition to the voluntary services already performed within the Sakha Sangha.

#### Clause 5

The Parichalak may, on his own initiative, directly enrol any devotee as a Regular Member of the Kendra Sangha and subsequently direct that person to become a member of any Sakha Sangha.

# REF-002-004 — Associate Members

## Subject

Associate Members

---

### Source Reference

REF-SRC-002

---

### Constitutional Provision

#### Clause 1

The Parichalak may, either on his own initiative or on the recommendation of a Sakha Sangha, enrol any person as an Associate Member after considering the person's active participation and sympathetic attitude toward the implementation of schemes undertaken by the Kendra Sangha.

#### Clause 2

An Associate Member is entitled to attend the functions of both the Sakha Sanghas and the Kendra Sangha.

#### Clause 3

An Associate Member is not entitled to contest elections for any elected office or to elect representatives to elected positions in either the Sakha Sangha or the Kendra Sangha.

# REF-002-005 — Cessation of Membership

## Subject

Cessation of Membership

---

### Source Reference

REF-SRC-002

---

### Constitutional Provision

#### Clause 1

Membership shall automatically cease upon the death of the member, failure to renew the annual Parichaya Patra or Anumati Patra, or absence from three consecutive weekly Sangha Pujas without the permission of the President of the concerned Sakha Sangha.

#### Clause 2

Membership of the Kendra Sangha shall automatically cease when membership in the concerned Sakha Sangha ceases, unless the Parichalak directs otherwise.

#### Clause 3

On receipt of a report from the President of a Sakha Sangha or any member of the Governing Body, membership may cease on grounds prescribed in the Bye-Laws.

#### Clause 4

Membership may cease for misdemeanour committed by the member.

#### Clause 5

Membership may cease for violating the Rules and Regulations of the Sakha Sangha or the Kendra Sangha.

#### Clause 6

Membership may cease for indulging in activities contrary to the ideologies of Shri Shri Thakur.

#### Clause 7

Membership may cease for reasons involving moral turpitude.

#### Clause 8

Membership may cease if the member subsequently loses faith in the ideologies of Shri Shri Thakur.

#### Clause 9

A person whose membership has ceased shall not be permitted to participate in any activity of the Kendra Sangha.
