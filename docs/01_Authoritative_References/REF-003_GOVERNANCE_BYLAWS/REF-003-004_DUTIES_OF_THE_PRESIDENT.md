---

### Constitutional Provision

#### Clause 1

The President shall ordinarily preside over all meetings of the Governing Body and all combined meetings of the Governing Body and the Advisory Board.

#### Clause 2

The President shall exercise general superintendence over the offices of the Kendra Sangha and their activities.

#### Clause 3

The President shall have the authority to inspect, as far as practicable, the daily Seva and Puja of Shri Shri Thakur at Puri to ensure the smooth functioning of the affairs of the Kendra Sangha.

#### Clause 4

The President shall perform such other functions as may be assigned under the By-Laws or entrusted by the Governing Body from time to time.
