### Constitutional Provision

#### Clause 1

The Governing Body shall consist of **nine members** elected in a combined meeting of the existing Governing Body and the Advisory Board convened by the Secretary of the Kendra Sangha for this purpose.

#### Clause 2

From among its members, the Governing Body shall elect the following office bearers:

- President
- Vice-President
- Parichalak
- Secretary
- Assistant Secretary
- Treasurer

#### Clause 3

The term of office of the members of the Governing Body, except the life members, shall be **three years** from the date they assume office.

#### Clause 4

If any of the life-member positions become vacant due to retirement or non-existence, the combined meeting of the Governing Body and the Advisory Board may fill the vacancy by unanimously selecting a suitable person.

#### Clause 5

Members of the Governing Body shall conduct themselves as humble servants of the Sangha (Sangha Sevi), maintaining uniformity in their conception, expression and action in accordance with the ideals of Shri Shri Thakur.
