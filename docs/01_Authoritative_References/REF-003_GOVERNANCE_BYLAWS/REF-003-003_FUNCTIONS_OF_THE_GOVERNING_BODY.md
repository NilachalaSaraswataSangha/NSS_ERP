### Constitutional Provision

#### Clause 1

The overall management of all the affairs of the Kendra Sangha is entrusted to the Governing Body. It is responsible for taking policy decisions in accordance with the ideals of Shri Shri Thakur and acts as the custodian of the movable and immovable properties of Nilachala Kutir, Smruti Mandir, and Sikshya Kendra.

#### Clause 2

The Governing Body shall administer and manage the properties, institutions, and affairs of the Kendra Sangha in furtherance of its objectives.

#### Clause 3

The Governing Body may frame rules and regulations for the proper management and maintenance of the properties and institutions under its control.

#### Clause 4

The Governing Body may entrust the duties of two or more office bearers to one person whenever it considers such an arrangement appropriate.

#### Clause 5

The Governing Body may constitute committees and sub-committees consisting of suitable members of the Kendra Sangha for specific purposes, including the organization of ceremonial functions.

#### Clause 6

The Governing Body shall ordinarily strive to take unanimous decisions in spiritual matters. Where an equal division of opinion makes a decision unavoidable, the President may exercise a casting vote.

#### Clause 7

The Governing Body may, as a temporary measure, take over the Seva and Puja of Shri Shri Thakur in any Sakha Sangha or Asana Mandir if it is satisfied that serious acts of omission or commission have resulted in a significant departure from the ideologies of Shri Shri Thakur. Any expenditure incurred by the Kendra Sangha from the Sinking Fund for this purpose shall subsequently be reimbursed by the concerned Sakha Sangha.

#### Clause 8

The Governing Body may undertake any other activities that it considers necessary and helpful for fulfilling the purpose for which Shri Shri Thakur established the Nilachala Saraswata Sangha, provided such action is approved through a unanimous resolution of the Governing Body.
